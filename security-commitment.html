Our Privacy Commitment

We built this tool for engineers who work with confidential client drawings. Your files are never stored, never read, never shared.

🗑️
Instant Deletion After Download
Your converted DXF file is permanently deleted from our server the moment you click Download. No copies are kept anywhere.
🧠
In-Memory Processing
Your PDF or image is processed entirely in RAM. It is never written to a permanent disk location and exists only for the duration of conversion.
🔐
No Third-Party Sharing
We do not use any cloud storage (S3, Google Drive, etc.) for your files. No third party ever sees your drawings.
🚫
No Account, No Tracking
We do not require sign-up. We use anonymous rate-limiting by IP. We do not build profiles of what you convert.
⏱️
10-Minute Auto-Purge
Even if you don't download immediately, a background cleaner purges all files older than 10 minutes automatically — no exceptions.
🔒
SSL/TLS Encryption in Transit
All data between your browser and our servers is encrypted with TLS. No one can intercept your drawings in transit.

⏱ What happens to your file, step by step

1
You select a file
The file stays in your browser until you click Convert. Nothing is uploaded yet.
2
Upload via encrypted connection
The file is sent over HTTPS/TLS directly to our conversion server. No CDN or third-party relay.
3
Conversion in RAM
PyMuPDF and ezdxf process your file entirely in memory. The DXF output is written to a temporary location with a random UUID filename.
4
You download the DXF
The file is streamed to your browser. The moment the stream completes, a background task immediately deletes the temporary file from disk.
5
Nothing remains
Your original file and the converted DXF are both gone. There is no record of the content of your drawings on our system.
